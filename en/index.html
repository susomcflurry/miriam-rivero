I interpret between German, English and Spanish at conferences, meetings and company visits, on site or remotely.

I also translate and adapt texts so they read as if they were written in the target language.

Planning a conference, a company visit or a meeting between teams from Germany and Spain? Tell me the date, place and languages and I’ll come back with a proposal. Get in touch

Experience

  1. Simultaneous DE → EN with streaming · Essen, 2025
  2. EN → ES translation and transcreation of scripts · Remote, since 2025
  3. Consecutive interpreting · Cologne, 2024

I prepare every assignment thoroughly: the glossary, the proper names and the agenda reach the booth before I do.

I took my Master’s in Conference Interpreting at TH Köln, and Cologne is where I started working, in the booth and in consecutive.

In 2025 I interpreted at the Rhine-Ruhr FISU World University Games, from German into English, live on the stream. Today I live in Madrid, where I also translate scripts from English into Spanish.
