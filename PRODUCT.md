## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (plus minimal vanilla JS if needed), no framework or build step. Proposed by Claude for a three-language landing and accepted by the owner. ## Users

- **Event organizers**: congresses, trade fairs and sports events who need booth or remote conference interpreters.
- **Translation and interpreting agencies**: looking for a reliable freelancer to add to their pool for DE/EN/ES assignments.
- **Companies**: they need meetings, site visits, negotiations, documents and marketing content handled across German, English and Spanish.
- **Private individuals**: they need documents translated or someone to accompany them to appointments.

The visitors usually arrive from a LinkedIn profile, a referral or a search. Within a minute they want to confirm that she covers their language pair, has real event experience and can be reached easily.

## Product Purpose

This is the personal professional site of **Miriam Rivero**, a freelance conference interpreter and translator. It presents her services and credentials so that clients get in touch with her. Success means that qualified enquiries reach her by email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

She is a **native German speaker based in Madrid** with a **Master's in Conference Interpreting from TH Köln**. She works in German, English and Spanish (DE ⇄ EN, ES and EN ⇄ ES) and has booth experience at international events. That makes her a German-native interpreter available on site in Spain, which is rare in the Madrid market. She also works remotely.

## Product Principles

1. **Language pairs and credentials up front.** A client must be able to check her fit in seconds.
2. **One way to act.** Every path leads to an email or LinkedIn contact.
3. **Show quality through the copy.** The text in all three languages is itself proof of the service.
4. **Use only real evidence.** It is better to be sparse than to embellish.
